# Roadmap

The current priority is keeping v0.1.x correct, documented, and reproducible. Possible later work includes broader live-provider validation, additional geometry topology handling, machine-readable output schemas, streaming acquisition, and carefully scoped adapters.

These are directions, not commitments. CityJSON, OpenStreetMap/Overpass, Overture-native ingestion, terrain/3D representations, deletion feeds, broader standards support, and application-specific integrations remain outside v0.1.0 until they have complete implementations, tests, licensing review, and maintenance capacity.
