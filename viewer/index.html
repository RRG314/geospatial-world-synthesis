What this demo does—and does not prove.

It demonstrates the real public data model, deterministic synthesis, evidence tracing, conflict retention, CRS normalization, and neutral exports on a small fictional dataset. It is not a full GIS client, universal conflation benchmark, cadastral authority, or safety-critical system.
